CROSSWORD: Learn and Play
A relaxing educational word puzzle game built in Unity, designed to help players expand their English vocabulary through interactive word search challenges.

Game Introduction
CROSSWORD: Learn and Play is a calm educational word puzzle game where players uncover advanced English vocabulary by finding hidden words inside a 10x10 letter grid. Using only the meaning of each word as a clue, players actively recall and apply their vocabulary knowledge.
Players read each definition, recall the matching word, and find it in the grid by clicking and dragging across letters in any of eight directions (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal). When a word is found correctly, its letters and its meaning are highlighted with a unique color, giving immediate visual feedback. The game ends when all ten words have been discovered.
The soft green aesthetic, simple drag-to-select controls, and stress-free atmosphere make the game ideal for focused learning and language growth.

Educational Concept
The pedagogical goal of the game is to expand the player's English vocabulary and strengthen reading comprehension, word recognition, spelling accuracy, and contextual understanding of advanced English terms. Players are introduced to meaningful words useful in academic, professional, and everyday communication, and each puzzle encourages them to learn definitions, identify word relationships, and remember spelling patterns through repeated interaction.
The game primarily applies the learning theory of Cognitivism, which focuses on how people process, organize, store, and recall information. Players actively connect definitions with vocabulary terms, recognize patterns between clues and answers, and reinforce memory through puzzle-solving repetition rather than passive reading.
The mechanics directly facilitate learning through interactive problem-solving. Clues provide only the definition, so players must analyze the meaning and actively recall the word before searching for it. For example, the clue "persistence and determination" leads the player to discover the word Tenacity. Immediate visual feedback from correctly found words reinforces learning, while repeated exposure to advanced vocabulary improves familiarity and confidence over time.
